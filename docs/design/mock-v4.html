How to win

Goal: finish each 8-hour shift ranked #1 on the leaderboard (top-right). Every vehicle, AI or human, scores the same way.

Points: +10 per pallet delivered, +40 for a P0 order. Half points if late. −15 if you cause a crash.

Your first minute
  1. Press 🚜 Drive (or click any vehicle, then Take the wheel).
  2. Follow the glowing ring. When the ring turns green and the bar says In range, you are close enough (within about 1.5 tiles, no need to be exact). Press E to pick up. Then drive to the blue bay ring and press E again to deliver.
  3. Pick contract cards when offered. They add bonus points and post purple pallets for you.
  4. Keep the battery above 20%. Park on a green charger and press E.
Controls
W / S forward and reverse · A / D steer · E pick, drop, charge · Esc hand back to the AI

Districts: cold storage drains the battery faster, the hazmat cage caps your speed, fast pick loads quickly and bulk floor stacks load slowly. Contracts into harder districts pay more.

Hitting another vehicle at speed is a crash: both stop for 4 minutes and a pallet may be damaged. Slow down near aisles and bays.

Growth: the floor fits 24 vehicles. Every 6 more (use + Agent in the Fleet pill) builds a new rack wing to the south with its own bays, chargers and districts, up to 4 wings. Order demand scales with fleet size.

Multiplayer: in the server build, other players take over other vehicles and appear on the same leaderboard. This static mock only knows about you.
